# Campaign workspace

- **Scope and mode:** Operate-mode desktop campaign workspace at `app/page.tsx`; wide desktop is the authored demo, with docked drawers at narrower widths and review-first behavior on phones.
- **Audience and job:** One professional graphic designer develops a campaign direction from a brief, three locked references, and sourced proposals. The primary task is to judge and organize references, not chat with an agent or generate finished advertisements.
- **Proof and content:** Static Bloom synthetic fragrance brief, three locked reference roles, palette and type specimens, sourced proposals with rights evidence, visual territories, and readable action receipts.
- **Chosen direction:** Paste-up Proofing Desk using the approved Working Mechanical comp at `.impeccable/mocks/decision/model-pick.webp`. This comp is the spatial contract for the first viewport.
- **Memorable moment:** A proposal in the right-side galley previews its destination, receives explicit designer approval, is visibly waxed down onto the center mechanical, and leaves an undoable receipt.
- **Topology:** Narrow semantic campaign job ticket left; dominant React Konva mechanical center; semantic Review Tray and quiet Agent Activity rail right. The center remains the largest continuous region at every editing breakpoint.
- **Interaction constraints:** Agent-found items default to review. Direct placement is an explicit scoped grant and lands in Agent Additions. Locked items cannot be materially altered without explicit approval. Canvas actions require keyboard-accessible DOM alternatives.
- **Material inventory:** semantic HTML for shell, ledger, tray, controls, receipts, and metadata; React Konva for spatial content; authored SVG for rulers, crop marks, registration targets, and annotations; generated raster assets for paper grain, tape texture, and the three synthetic locked references; no rasterized interface text.
- **Implementation anchors:** approved comp size 1536×1024; sampled wheat paper `#D0C7BA`, darker work surface `#B3A590`, chrome/ink `#171717`, non-photo blue near `#4779B8`, approval green near `#217A3A`, muted ruby near `#A05040`. Verify net rendered colors against same-size screenshots.
- **Open implementation decisions:** final freely embeddable condensed grotesk and production-label typefaces, formal accessibility conformance target, exact narrower-desktop cutoff, and whether Postgres/R2 begin as real services or local adapters behind production interfaces.
