# Graphic Designer Campaign Workflow

This workflow shows how a graphic designer can direct a professional campaign through conversation while Iterum exposes structured, reviewable actions through WebMCP. ChatGPT can prepare and propose work, but the designer remains responsible for locking the brief, approving references and directions, and accepting final applications.

| Stage | What the designer says to ChatGPT | What Iterum/WebMCP does | Designer decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brief | “Create a campaign for a mineral floral fragrance launching in October.” | Creates the campaign, audience, positioning, deliverables, mandatories, and exclusions. | Edit and lock the brief. |
| Client material | “Add these bottle shots, logo files, and three references as protected assets.” | Uploads and identifies the assets, preserving filenames and sources. | Mark assets as mandatory or optional. |
| Research | “Find references for wet concrete, preserved flowers, violet resin, and severe condensed typography.” | Searches Pexels, captures URLs, creates web clips, and proposes tags. | Approve or reject the findings. |
| Organization | “Divide the approved references into three genuinely different directions.” | Groups references, assigns territories, adds rationale notes, and proposes layouts. | Preview and approve each Direction Draft. |
| Color | “Extract colors from these references. Keep the sodium amber and propose cooler counterpoints.” | Extracts local palettes, queries The Color API, and optionally explores Colormind variations. | Pin the final campaign palette. |
| Typography | “Find an editorial display face and a restrained grotesk body face.” | Searches free fonts, labels commercial recommendations, and creates live specimens. | Approve the pairing. |
| Direction | “Develop the Mineral Afterimage route into the lead campaign direction.” | Consolidates composition, image treatment, typography, palette, tone, and usage rules. | Approve the final direction. |
| Applications | “Apply this direction to a poster, Instagram story, landing-page hero, and launch invitation.” | Generates reviewable campaign applications using the approved system. | Revise and approve each application. |
| Presentation | “Prepare the client presentation.” | Builds a clean direction deck with rationale, sources, palette, typography, and applications. | Export a PDF or share link. |

## Operating principle

Iterum should make agent work inspectable and reversible. Discovery, organization, color, typography, direction development, and application generation enter a review state before becoming canonical campaign material. The designer provides the final creative judgment at every consequential transition.
